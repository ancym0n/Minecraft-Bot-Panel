Privacy Policy

Last updated:

1. How MCBP Handles Your Data

MCBP is a desktop app that runs on your own computer. It has no servers, no user accounts, and no analytics. It does not send any information to the developer, and nothing you enter in the app ever leaves your device except for the connections described below.

2. What Is Stored on Your Device

The username or email you log in with is kept in memory while the app is open. When you sign in with a Microsoft account, Mineflayer's login library caches your authentication tokens on your computer so you do not have to enter a device code every time you connect. If you click Later on an update prompt, that skipped version number is saved in the app window's local storage. Any scripts or styles you add to custom/scripts/ and custom/theme.css stay in local files on your computer.

3. Network Connections

When you click Connect, the app connects directly to the Minecraft server address you entered. If you use Microsoft mode, it also talks to official Microsoft and Xbox Live login servers to authenticate your account. The app window loads the Nunito and Poppins fonts from Google Fonts, so Google can see your IP address when it opens.

On launch and when you click Sync Versions, MCBP checks registry.npmjs.org and downloads newer releases of mineflayer, minecraft-data, and mineflayer-pathfinder into its local mineflayer-runtime folder. It also checks GitHub Releases to let you know when a new version of MCBP is out (updates are never forced).

4. Custom Scripts

Scripts placed inside custom/scripts/ run locally on your computer with Node.js access. Only add custom scripts that you wrote yourself or that come from a source you trust and have checked first.

5. This Website

This site does not use tracking cookies or analytics. Your browser loads fonts from Google Fonts, and the page queries the GitHub API, shields.io, and raw GitHub content for the latest release packages, download count, star count, and supported Minecraft version range, so those services can see your IP address. The Download Manager caches the latest release metadata in your browser's local storage, and the last updated date is cached in session storage until you close the tab.

6. Summary

In short, MCBP guarantees the following:

  • No Data Collection or Sale: Nothing from the app is collected, sold, or shared with third parties.
  • No Tracking: The app has no analytics, advertising, or background tracking.
  • You Stay in Control: All saved data is in files on your device, and the full source code is open on GitHub.

7. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the app changes how it handles data. Any updates will be published on this page.
