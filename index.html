FAQ

1. What is your Privacy Policy?

MCBP respects your privacy. The app runs on your computer, has no analytics, and does not send anything to the developer. Your settings, custom scripts, and cached login tokens stay on your device and are only used to connect your bot to Minecraft servers.

View Privacy Policy

2. What is MCBP?

MCBP stands for Minecraft Bot Panel. It is a desktop app for creating and managing Minecraft Java Edition bots, toggling built-in automation scripts, and running your own custom Mineflayer scripts from a simple dashboard.

3. What are its main features?

MCBP includes Microsoft and Offline login, automatic Mineflayer Version Sync for new Minecraft updates, six built-in scripts (AntiAFK, ClearInv, Follow, GoTo, Mine, and Spam), live server Chat and Logs panels, and support for custom .js scripts and theme.css styles.

4. How do I use it or write custom scripts?

The app pairs an Electron dashboard with mineflayer and mineflayer-pathfinder. You can toggle built-in scripts right from the panel, drop your own .js files into custom/scripts/, or restyle the app with custom/theme.css.

View Documentation

5. Which operating systems and Minecraft versions are supported?

MCBP runs on both Windows and Linux without needing Node.js installed. It supports Minecraft Java Edition from 1.8 up to the newest release supported by PrismarineJS, and leaving the version input on Auto detects the server's version automatically.
