FAQ

1. What is MCBP?

MCBP is short for Minecraft Bot Panel. It's a desktop app that lets you run a Minecraft Java Edition bot without writing any code. Log in, join a server, and then drive it yourself, flip on one of the built-in scripts, or just tell it what to do in chat.

2. What can it actually do?

Quite a lot. You get Microsoft and Offline login, WASD control with a live radar of nearby players, mobs and signs, and a 46-slot inventory that also shows chests, shops and server menus you can click through. On top of that there are 26 built-in scripts: farming, fishing, mining, tunneling, building, chest looting, auto-eat, auto-armor, KillAura, Guard, Follow, AutoReconnect, a full GearUp from wood to diamond, a Survival mode that just plays on its own, and more. Chat comes with all its colors, plus ! commands for the bot.

3. Does it have AI?

Yep, and it runs on your own PC, no API keys or subscriptions. Click Auto-Setup in the AI tab and it grabs Ollama and a model that fits your hardware. The bot can then chat like a normal player (paced so anti-spam plugins leave it alone), and with Actions on, players can ask it to "follow me" or "mine 10 oak logs" and it'll actually do it. You decide who's allowed to give it orders.

4. Can I make my own scripts?

Sure. The Studio tab has templates, the Mineflayer docs built right in, and can write a script for you from a plain description using your local AI. Or drop your own .js files into custom/scripts/. Want different colors? Edit custom/theme.css.

5. Which systems and Minecraft versions work?

Windows, Linux (deb, rpm, pacman, AppImage or tar.gz) and macOS (Intel and Apple Silicon). You don't need Node.js. The Download button picks the right file for you. Minecraft Java Edition works from 1.8 up to the newest version Mineflayer supports, and the app keeps Mineflayer updated on its own, so new versions usually work without an MCBP update. Leave the version on Auto and it'll detect it.

6. Is it free? What about my data?

It's free and open source (GPL-3.0). There's no analytics and nothing gets sent to me. Your logins, settings, scripts, chat logs and AI models all stay on your computer.
